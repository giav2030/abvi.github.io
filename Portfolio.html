About

Gerardo Ivan Abogado Villamil is a multidisciplinary artist born and raised in Mexico City, now based in New York. From an early age, he has been exploring music theory, composition, piano and guitar playing. Later on, moved by his curiosity to learn the technology applied to music production, he pursued his undergraduate degree in Digital Music Production Engineering in Instituto Tecnológico y de Estudios Superiores de Monterrey (Tec de Monterrey) at Mexico City Campus. The years as an undergrad allowed him to get hands-on experience in the world of sound engineering, from signal flow and processing inside a professional recording studio to experimenting with microcontroller devices that allowed the communication between computer, DAWs and instruments via MIDI and scripting. In the same way, to understand the physical nuances of sound theory. Reaching the end of this degree, Ivan had the opportunity to study abroad for a one-year Sound Design for Visual Media program at Vancouver Film School (VFS). This experience allowed him to dive deeper into the pipeline for sound recording, editing, designing and mixing for the film industry standards. Moreover, he was able to explore the vast possibilities of video games sound implementation within the world of game engines such as Unity and Unreal Engine.

After graduating from both programs, he got the opportunity to work side by side with film industry professionals at the post production studio Labo, located in Coyoacán, Mexico City. With 5+ years of experience, Ivan was able to fully understand a professional sound design pipeline from sound assistant, sound editor and later re-recording mixer. During this time, he was able to collaborate in projects for unique talents such as David Pablos, Kike Maíllo, Gabriel and Rodolfo Riva Palacios, Grupo Chespirito and Jorge Riquelme Serrano to name a few. Some of these projects were showcased in national theaters as well as his clients' streaming platforms such as Netflix, Warner (HBO Max), Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Paramount and Vix.

After experiencing the never ending growth of technology in the field, he decided to delve back into the world of engineering and innovation in the entertainment industry and beyond by coursing his master of professional studies degree in the Interactive Telecommunications Program (ITP) in Tisch School of the Arts at New York University (NYU). Now, he is using technology as his raw material, delving into the next steps of audiovisual production, merging the digital and physical worlds with interactive devices/experiences, as well as sculpting data as visual experiences. His goal is to master the tools that allow him to collaborate in innovating the way we experience sound, music, film and art.

This website is a living example of his philosophy: Life is based on connections, we are all connected between each other as well as with our surroundings. There is a logic and a sense of randomness within that logic. We don't know which point will connect us to the next. A new person we meet, or an opportunity we get, even an out of nowhere event. Everything is connected, and happens for a reason. That's what I believe. That is where structure meets the unexpected.
